# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Refer to that document for the complete text, which this project adopts.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Be honest about what the software and its results can and cannot show.
- Protect privacy. Never share anyone's personal or health information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere. Report concerns privately to the project
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation, in response to violations. The
enforcement guidelines follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
